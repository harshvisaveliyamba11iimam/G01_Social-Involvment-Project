Greetings from the Indian Institute of Management Amritsar!

We, the student participants of **Group 01, MBA Batch 2025–27**, have successfully completed our mandatory **Social Involvement Project (SIP)** conducted in collaboration with **Punjabi Samvad NGO, Amritsar**.

Our project, titled **“Threads, Hands and Heritage of Amritsar: Documenting, Analyzing, and Preserving the Living Craft Traditions of Local Artisans”**, focused on understanding the socio-economic challenges, intergenerational vulnerabilities, and technological transitions faced by the heritage artisan communities of Amritsar and its surrounding areas (including the UNESCO-recognized Thathera metalworkers of Jandyala Guru, sacred Kirpan sword forgers, Partition-legacy clay tandoor makers, classical tabla makers, and master wooden chess piece carvers). 

As a tangible outcome of this collaboration and to uphold Punjabi Samvad's guiding philosophy of **“Dialogue. Dignity. Change.”**, our team has developed and published a dedicated public digital narrative archive and website celebrating these artisans and prominently highlighting Punjabi Samvad’s commendable heritage preservation initiatives:
