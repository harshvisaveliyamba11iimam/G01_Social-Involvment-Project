Certificate of Internship
To Whomsoever It May Concern

This is to certify that Mr. Visaveliya Harsh Jayantibhai, S/O Mr. Jayantibhai Visaveliya, a student of MBA, Batch 2025–27, Indian Institute of Management Amritsar, has successfully completed his Social Involvement Project (SIP) in association with Punjabi Samvad, Amritsar.

The project titled “Threads, Hands and Heritage of Amritsar: Documenting, Analyzing, and Preserving the Living Craft Traditions of Local Artisans” was undertaken with the objective of exploring and documenting the socio-economic conditions of traditional artisan communities in Amritsar, identifying key challenges related to raw material accessibility, market disruption, and generational continuity, and building public visibility to support heritage preservation aligned with Punjabi Samvad’s mission of “Dialogue. Dignity. Change.”

As part of the project, Mr. Visaveliya Harsh Jayantibhai actively contributed to the overall project activities, including on-ground field visits, qualitative interviews with master craftspeople across Amritsar and Jandyala Guru, transcription and analysis of artisan narratives, assessment of livelihood vulnerabilities, and the development of an interactive digital storytelling platform to showcase Amritsar's handicraft heritage to a wider audience.

Throughout the project, he demonstrated sincerity, commitment, teamwork, professionalism, and a strong sense of social responsibility. His valuable contribution towards understanding the identified social issues and recommending practical interventions for supporting local artisans was highly appreciated.

We hereby acknowledge the successful completion of his Social Involvement Project with Punjabi Samvad and appreciate his valuable contribution towards the project.

Warm regards,
Jyoti Bawa
